VERA — rework demos

Twelve pages plus Home, four size tiers each. Every page is a view over one dataset — the same document appears as a row in Proposals with four askers, then in Documents, then on a list, then inside a project's planned-versus-delivered numbers. The rules they satisfy are in docs/design/shared/; what is decided and what is waiting for review is in docs/demos/STATUS.md.

A tier is a display, not a column count. notebook 1440×900 at a 16px root is what everything is authored at — it fits a laptop. large is 1920×1200 at 15px: a bigger screen and a step back, so you see more at once. small (tablet, 1024×768) and phone keep notebook's type on a physically smaller panel — the layout gives way, the type does not shrink. Small still shows both columns; only the phone turns the detail pane into a sheet.
Two shapes. Most pages are master–detail — a list you scan beside a pane that answers in full, which is the originals' shape and the one that can say why a row is where it is — Documents, Proposals, Validations, Lists, Categories, Users & Groups and Hooks. At notebook and large the list half reads as a table. Projects and Intentions are cards, because there are only a handful at once and each answers will we make it. Tasks, Schedules and Plugins are tables, where you compare rows across. Home has no list and no record, so it is cards, one per entry you hold.
Two demo-only controls, bottom of every page. size (right) jumps between the four tiers of the same page. as (left) re-renders it as a different actor — entries and actions the actor cannot reach are absent, never disabled, which is easier to see than to describe. Try Proposals as curator and as admin: the curator sees only what is in their proposal custody.
Sizes are shown at their size. small and phone open inside a device frame — drawn at 1024×768 and 393×852 on a backdrop, the phone with a phone's bezel — because a tablet layout stretched across a desktop is not a tablet layout. If the device is taller than your window it is scaled, never cropped: a phone cut off at 700px would make every judgement about what fits above the fold wrong. notebook and large just fill the window; notebook is the default and large exists to use extra room when there is extra room.

What to look at first

Proposals — one row per document: Národní listy has four asks and is decided once. As curator the queue is what the Custody rules placed in Jana's proposal custodies; the Placed in card says which. A proposal made after its project's approval carries the flag and the project's priority, pre-filled.
Projects — decision and period are two badges. Brněnské kalendáře is Proposed: Approve… picks the priority, approves two and skips the one with a copy. Hudebniny has nothing in it, so its manager can still delete it.
Documents Validations — outstanding shows D \ A, never what was already accepted. The Forbidden filter holds what a rule forbade; a curator can only message an admin about it.
Digitization Lists — Mark Done… is an act, and an item can be marked could not be digitized first. Regional weeklies has an item blocked since listing: it stays on, flagged.
Categories — the mix of one intention, picked in Mix of. NDK Periodika tracks Periodicals push — watch: Regional is 55% of Periodicals. Saving the house mix lists the seven intentions that track it, and unticked ones keep the old mix.
Users & Groups — Roles · Users · Groups and proposal custodies. A solid line is a role set by hand, a dashed one was mapped by an Identity plugin at login. Groups never carry a role.
